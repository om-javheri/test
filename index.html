Our Heritage

Founded on a passion for authentic flavors, Amber Bistro has been a local staple for over ten years. We believe in the magic of a shared meal.

From our open kitchen to your table, we strive to create a warm, inviting atmosphere for every guest who walks through our doors.

We take pride in sourcing ingredients from local farmers, ensuring that every plate supports our community and tastes incredible.
